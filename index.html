About Me

I work at the intersection of business operations and automation.

My background includes virtual assistance, sales and lead generation, business research, customer communication, social media support, and AI automation.

Working inside day-to-day business processes has helped me understand where teams lose time through repetitive tasks, manual follow-ups, disconnected information, and unnecessary administrative work.
