FLAGSHIP SYSTEM 01
Decision Operations • Executive Search Demo

Decision Leverage Engine

A decision-support workflow designed to separate routine decisions from leadership decisions, prepare decision context, surface missing information and risks, and protect senior leadership attention without allowing AI to become the executive.

Built MVP
Decision Intake→ AI Analysis→ Routine / Material / Critical→ Escalation Logic→ Human Approval→ Decision Log
AI prepares judgment. Humans exercise judgment.

Before

  • Routine issues are escalated upward.
  • Leaders repeatedly reconstruct context.
  • Risks and missing information are inconsistent.
  • Operational decisions consume leadership attention.
  • Decision reasoning is difficult to review later.

After

  • Decision context is structured before review.
  • Routine matters remain within appropriate authority.
  • High-impact decisions reach leadership with a concise brief.
  • Human approval remains required for important decisions.
  • Decision history and human reasoning are logged.
Business ProblemToo many routine decisions compete with higher-value leadership work.
System DesignStructured intake, AI reasoning, escalation logic, human approval, and auditable logging.
Designed to ImproveLeadership capacity • Decision preparation • Escalation quality • Documentation
Primary KPI: Leadership Escalation RateHuman OverridesDecision ConfidenceLeadership Attention Recovered
Demo project built to demonstrate AI automation and business-process improvement capabilities. KPI definitions and outcomes shown are design objectives and test frameworks, not claimed client results.
FLAGSHIP SYSTEM 02
Signal Operations • Executive Search Demo

Executive Signal Firewall

A signal-routing system designed to protect high-value leadership attention while making sure important client risks, candidate issues, revenue opportunities, operational problems, and internal requests reach the right owner at the right time.

Built MVP
Signal Intake→ AI Classification→ Deterministic Guardrail→ IGNORE / LOG / DELEGATE / ESCALATE→ Human Review→ Signal Log
BEFORE
AFTER
Final n8n MVP: structured AI analysis + deterministic routing guardrail + human escalation + same-row audit logging.
Business ProblemImportant and unimportant signals arrive together, creating leadership noise and missed-risk potential.
System DesignAI classification, deterministic guardrails, four routing actions, human escalation, and Google Sheets audit logging.
Designed to ImproveSignal quality • Ownership clarity • Escalation speed • Leadership focus • Opportunity visibility
Leadership Signal Escalation RateHuman OverridesCritical Risks DetectedLeadership Interruptions Avoided
Demo project validated with mock executive-search scenarios. The workflow successfully routes IGNORE, LOG, DELEGATE, and ESCALATE signals and captures human response data. No client performance results are claimed.
