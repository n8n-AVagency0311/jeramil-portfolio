02
Signal Operations · Executive Search Demo Built MVP

Executive Signal Firewall

A signal-routing system designed to protect high-value leadership attention while making sure important client risks, candidate issues, revenue opportunities, operational problems and internal requests reach the right owner at the right time.

Fewer interruptions. Better ones.

Business problem

Important and unimportant signals arrive together, creating leadership noise and missed-risk potential.

System design

AI classification, deterministic guardrails, four routing actions, human escalation and Google Sheets audit logging.

Designed to improve

Signal quality · Ownership clarity · Escalation speed · Leadership focus · Opportunity visibility

  • Routine issues interrupt leadership

Final n8n MVP: structured AI analysis + deterministic routing guardrail + human escalation + same-row audit logging.

Demo project validated with mock executive-search scenarios. The workflow successfully routes IGNORE, LOG, DELEGATE and ESCALATE signals and captures human response data. No client performance results are claimed.

About

I work at the intersection of business operations, human judgment and automation.

My background includes sales and lead generation, business research, client communication, virtual assistance, customer support and hands-on automation work. That operational experience helps me see where businesses lose capacity through repetitive work, unclear ownership, disconnected information, manual follow-up and unnecessary escalation.

My goal is not to replace people with automation. It is to improve the system around them, so human attention is used where it creates the most value.

Straight answers

The questions I get asked first.

Do I have to change the tools I already use?

Almost never. I build around what you have — your CRM, your ATS, Google Workspace, your inbox. If something genuinely needs replacing, I'll tell you why and let you decide.

How long does a build take?

A single system is typically one to two weeks from scoping to handover. You'll get a date before I start, not after. For selected early clients I can start with a focused 7-day improvement project.

Will AI be making decisions about my business?

No. AI interprets and prepares; deterministic rules bound it; humans approve anything high-impact. Every system I build logs the decision and the reasoning behind it.

What does it cost?

Quoted per build, after scoping, fixed in writing before any work starts. No hourly surprises. If the honest answer is that you don't need me yet, I'll say that instead.
