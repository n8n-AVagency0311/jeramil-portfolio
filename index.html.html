01
Decision Operations · Executive Search Demo

Decision Leverage Engine

A decision-support workflow designed to separate routine decisions from leadership decisions, prepare context, surface missing information and risks, and protect senior leadership attention.

Business problem

Routine issues compete with higher-value leadership work, while important decisions often arrive without consistent context.

System design

Structured intake, AI analysis, escalation logic, human approval, and auditable logging.

Designed to improve

Leadership capacity, decision preparation, escalation quality, and documentation.

Decision flow
Decision Intake→AI Analysis→Routine / Material / Critical→Escalation Logic→Human Approval→Decision Log
Primary KPI: Leadership Escalation RateHuman OverridesDecision ConfidenceLeadership Attention Recovered

Demo project. KPI definitions and outcomes are design objectives and test frameworks, not claimed client results.

02
Signal Operations · Executive Search Demo

Executive Signal Firewall

A signal-routing system designed to protect high-value leadership attention while making sure client risks, candidate issues, revenue opportunities, operational problems, and internal requests reach the right owner at the right time.

Business problem

Important and unimportant signals arrive together, creating leadership noise and missed-risk potential.

System design

AI classification, deterministic guardrails, four routing actions, human escalation, and same-row audit logging.

Designed to improve

Signal quality, ownership clarity, escalation speed, leadership focus, and opportunity visibility.

Signal flow
Signal Intake→AI Classification→Deterministic Guardrail→IGNORE / LOG / DELEGATE / ESCALATE→Human Review→Signal Log
Leadership Signal Escalation RateHuman OverridesCritical Risks DetectedLeadership Interruptions Avoided

Validated with mock executive-search scenarios. The MVP routes IGNORE, LOG, DELEGATE, and ESCALATE signals and captures human response data. No client performance results are claimed.
